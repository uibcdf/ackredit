# Product roadmap continuation

## What

The maintainer accepted the review of Ackredit's original vision, implemented
core and remaining external-user/platform experience. The roadmap must retain
completed evidence while incorporating all six remaining fronts and their
execution order. This issue owns the planning update, not implementation of
every future feature.

## How

Refresh theme C's released application/experimental receiving status and F's
review against that available adoption. Extend G with standalone inert producer
validation, third-party guidance and explicit observation-scope decisions.
Add themes I–N for portable CLI/composition, coverage/reference origin/gaps,
MOLI boundaries, complete lifecycle cost, reference-manager interoperability
and non-bibliographic acknowledgement scope. Align maintained status and
retain source/public/release/stability/guide/adoption boundaries.

## Why

Precise references, invisible normal operation and useful later reports are the
original product goal. Core and receiving qualification do not alone complete
the saved-result CLI, explain instrumentation coverage, accept platform
contracts, measure cold-start/dependency cost or decide acknowledgement scope.

## What was refuted

Do not reopen resolved #111 or treat every consumer's integration as a blocker
for the original real-adoption milestone. Do not promote provisional APIs,
infer cumulative speedup, represent planned tools as implemented, or turn all
optional future work into new prerequisites for 1.0.

## Scope and exclusions

English documentation only. No code, dependency, schema, current public file,
version/tag, synchronized guide or sibling source changes. Ackredit owns its
implementation; MolSysSuite #97 and MOLI #46 retain shared/platform decisions.
Focused owner issues are opened before new implementation begins.

## Acceptance criteria

- The canonical roadmap covers every accepted front with observable exit
  criteria, explicit decisions, owners and a concrete execution order.
- Completed historical evidence remains dated; source improvements and future
  public release qualification stay separate.
- Maintained status agrees with the roadmap and known closed/open receiving
  owner issues; site inclusion uses the canonical roadmap.
- Applicable reporting/index, documentation/links and synchronized-guide checks
  pass; archive this planning record and close #100 on delivery.
